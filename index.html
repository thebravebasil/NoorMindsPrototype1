Our Mission

To welcome Muslim youth into robotics and help them build career skills.

About Us

  • We are a new, Muslim-run FTC team starting this year.
  • Our team has elementary, middle, and high school students.
  • We meet every Sunday at our mosque.
  • We work with the team Photon Phoenix.
  • Mentors and parent volunteers help guide us.

Season Goal

Build a working robot and win by giving every student a role and working as a team.
